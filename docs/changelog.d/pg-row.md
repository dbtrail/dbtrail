- **PostgreSQL: the row at a moment and its history take a table that is not at `REPLICA IDENTITY FULL`** (part of #2775). Nothing captures such a change yet. `reconstruct` of one row, its history and the port's read of a row as of a time no longer refuse a change whose earlier row was captured with its key only: they build the row from what each change leaves. A large value an UPDATE left as it was is the one the row held before that change; with no row to take it from, the read refuses and says why. It is never NULL in the answer. Undo still needs the whole earlier row.
