- **On the PostgreSQL port, reading the copy's list of columns no longer waits for an update of the recent changes** (#3223). The list, and the checks of a `SET`, read no table's rows, but they waited for any update of the copy's recent changes that was running, holding their place on the copy. A statement the port then sent to the source had already paid that wait. With updates of 5 s running, the slowest 1 % of such statements went from 5 s to 44 ms. The list still runs on a worker of the copy, so a busy copy is refused as before, and it stays under the server's lag limit.
