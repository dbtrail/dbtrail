- **Engine Routing answers `DATE_SUB` and `SUBDATE` with an `INTERVAL` on the copy** (#2611). `WHERE created_at >= DATE_SUB(NOW(), INTERVAL 30 DAY)` went to MySQL every time: the copy's own `date_sub` counts the units between two dates, so it refused MySQL's call. The routing port now sends it as a function of the copy's session that subtracts the interval, when the answer is MySQL's: over a `DATETIME` or `TIMESTAMP` column without fractional seconds (a `TIMESTAMP` under UTC or a fixed offset), `NOW()`, `UTC_TIMESTAMP()` or `CURRENT_TIMESTAMP`, or a `DATE` stepped by seconds, minutes or hours, with a whole number and a unit from `SECOND` to `YEAR`. A `DATE` stepped by a day or more (a `DATE` on MySQL, a date and time on the copy), a fraction, `MICROSECOND`, a unit of two parts, a placeholder, text and a result outside the years 1 to 9999 stay on MySQL. The port without routing is unchanged. See [date functions under routing](time-travel-sql.md).
