- **Engine Routing answers `LENGTH` and `CHAR_LENGTH` of a text column from the copy, with MySQL's numbers** (#2918). Under a connection collation that compares otherwise than the copy (`utf8mb4_general_ci`, the default of the MariaDB client and of go-sql-driver), a statement with `LENGTH`, `OCTET_LENGTH`, `BIT_LENGTH`, `CHAR_LENGTH` or `CHARACTER_LENGTH` went to MySQL, counted as `session_differs`, although each returns a number the collation cannot change, so a report such as `SELECT k % 100 AS bucket, COUNT(*), SUM(LENGTH(c)) FROM sbtest1 GROUP BY bucket` ran as a full scan on the source. The copy now answers them under any collation. Where the copy did answer them, `LENGTH` counted characters and not bytes: over a `utf8mb4_bin` column holding `é` it said 1 where MySQL says 2, and `OCTET_LENGTH` of a text failed there. The port now sends `LENGTH` and `OCTET_LENGTH` as the copy's count of bytes, and the copy answers the five only when each one's argument is a `CHAR`, `VARCHAR` or `TEXT` column in `utf8mb4`, `utf8mb3` or `ascii`; over a `latin1`, binary or `JSON` column, an expression or a literal they stay on MySQL. `AVG(LENGTH(c))` still goes to MySQL, as every `AVG` has since 0.133.0 (#2806), and that, not the collation, is why the report in #2918 was forwarded under `utf8mb4_unicode_ci` too: on MariaDB 10.11 that collation does compare like the copy. See [the length functions on the copy](time-travel-sql.md).
