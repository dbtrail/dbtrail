- **PostgreSQL port: common reporting statements are answered by the copy with PostgreSQL's types** (#3316). `avg()` of a name a `WITH` defines as a numeric (`avg(spent)` of `sum(amount) AS spent`) is answered by the copy instead of the source. `sum()` of a `smallint` or an `integer` is announced as a `bigint`, as on PostgreSQL, instead of a `numeric`, so drivers return a whole number rather than a decimal. A `varchar(n)` or `char(n)` column returned as it is is announced with its type and length rather than as `text`. Unnamed `avg()`, `sum()` and divisions the copy answers are headed `avg`, `sum` and `?column?`. Closes #3219.
