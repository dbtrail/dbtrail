- **PostgreSQL port: a text column under a nondeterministic collation is the source's when a statement uses it** (#3111). Under an ICU collation that ignores case, PostgreSQL finds `'ab'` and `'AB'` equal and the copy compared bytes, so `WHERE t = 'AB'` gave 1 row instead of 3 with no notice, even with a forwarding user. The snapshot now records each column's collation, and a statement that compares, groups, joins, sorts, aggregates or passes such a column to a function goes to the source, or is refused without a forwarding user; showing it stays on the copy. For a snapshot taken before this version, the port reads the collations from the source's catalog through the forwarding user; without one, every text column of that snapshot is treated as nondeterministic until its next snapshot, so statements that compare or sort its text are refused until then.
