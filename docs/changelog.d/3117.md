- **PostgreSQL capture comes back by itself after a source outage of any length** (#3117). A source that could not be reached for more than 6 hours used to be left stopped until someone pressed Start, while its replication slot went on holding WAL on the source. Capture now keeps retrying, every 5 minutes once the wait has grown to that, and resumes from where it stopped when the source is back. Meanwhile the server's status stays failed and says that the source cannot be reached, for how long and why, and what to do if its address changed (**Edit** the server and save the new **Source host**), and the `capture_stopped` webhook still fires. Only connection failures and a server that is stopping or starting count as an outage. A refused password, a missing database or publication, a lost slot, exhausted connection slots, a failure of the index, or a source that was never captured (its address may simply be wrong) keep the 6-hour limit. A capture still connecting no longer shows as running, which also kept that webhook from firing during an outage. MySQL and MariaDB sources are not affected.
