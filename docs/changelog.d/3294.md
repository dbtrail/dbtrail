- **MySQL port: an expensive statement is answered by the copy even where its answer may differ from MySQL's** (#3294). **Upgrading: this changes what a client sees.** A `SELECT` sent as text (not prepared) whose plan on the source is expensive now goes to the copy even when the router would have sent it to MySQL for how the copy computes, sorts or prints a value: a `LIKE`, a `/` or an `AVG`, a `UNION`, a `CONCAT`, `WEEK`, `INSTR`, a `SUBSTRING` position, a date column read as a number, text compared or sorted, a `FLOAT` printed with fixed decimals. Such an answer can differ: other decimals, another column type, text sorted by bytes rather than by the column's collation (under `LIMIT`, other rows), a `LIKE` that tells letter case apart, ties in another order. These are counted under the new reason `heavy_on_copy`. A statement the copy cannot run still goes to MySQL without the client seeing the copy's error. Cheap statements, prepared statements, statements inside a transaction, statements under a session time zone other than UTC, and constructs the copy would read as another statement or that need the live server (`REGEXP`, `GROUP_CONCAT`, variables, the clock, `RAND`, JSON, locking reads and the rest listed in docs/time-travel-sql.md) are decided as before. A cheap statement with one of the relaxed constructs now costs one `EXPLAIN` on the source. For MySQL's own answer to an expensive statement, send it inside a transaction or as a prepared statement.
