- **The PostgreSQL port writes `round()` and `avg()` with PostgreSQL's decimals on every connection** (#3018). Without a forwarding user, the copy answered them with its own types: `round(avg(amount), 2)` of a `numeric` came back as `500` where PostgreSQL writes `500.00`, `round(x, 3)` of a `numeric(10,2)` as `12.50` (PostgreSQL: `12.500`), `round(qty, 2)` as `5` (`5.00`). Now `round(x, k)`, `round(x, -k)`, `round(x)` of a numeric, `round(avg(x))` and `round(a / b)` are answered as PostgreSQL answers them on every connection, and a `CASE` or `COALESCE` around one is checked value by value. What the copy would still answer otherwise (`avg()` of a numeric, `round()` of a `double precision` or of a whole number, a rounded value beside `UNION`, `greatest`, `least`, `nullif` or a `lag` default) goes to the source with a forwarding user, and without one is refused with error `0A000`, never answered with other digits.
