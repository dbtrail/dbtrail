- **The snapshot schedule asks a PostgreSQL source whether capture is caught up** (#2844). When nothing was indexed since an old snapshot, the schedule asks the source before it decides; for a PostgreSQL server it did not, and gave "PostgreSQL sources are not compared yet" as the reason whatever was the case. It now compares the capture's saved position with how far the source's WAL is flushed: two equal positions confirm the capture is caught up, and anything else is said as what it is on the Snapshots page (the source ahead, a capture that stopped saving, a capture past its source, a loss on record), the capture that stopped and the capture past its source as warnings in the log too. No answer of this probe reads a PostgreSQL source in full: its WAL moves for the whole PostgreSQL server, so a source that is ahead is how a healthy capture reads. A loss on record still leads to one full read, as before, through the update that the gap refuses.
