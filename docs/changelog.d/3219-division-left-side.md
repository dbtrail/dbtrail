- **PostgreSQL port: a division whose left side is a product no longer gets another answer from the copy** (#3219). The port let the copy divide by a whole number after looking only at the name just before the slash, while PostgreSQL divides the whole product. `SELECT sum(big) * qty / 2` with `big` a `bigint` answered `13` where PostgreSQL answers `13.5000000000000000`, and `amount * qty / 2` with `amount` a `numeric` lost PostgreSQL's decimals. The copy now divides as written only a product of columns of whole numbers or of floats and whole numbers written in the statement; any other goes to the source. MySQL and MariaDB sources are not affected.
