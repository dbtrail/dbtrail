- **The PostgreSQL port gives the copy one worker more, kept for short statements** (#3266). With reports holding every worker, a short statement for the copy waited behind them: a sum of one warehouse, a second alone, took 13 to 31 seconds under 30 connections. The copy now runs up to `--workers` plus 1 statements at once. A statement whose plan on the source reads one table and joins nothing takes any free worker, the extra one included; the reports, and every statement with no plan, run as before, at most `--workers` at once. "One table" is counted on the safe side: a subquery, a `WITH` or a partitioned table the plan keeps apart, or a join, is not short. The extra statement has its own `--threads` and `--sql-memory`; to spend no more than before, give the router one worker less. `/status` says how it stands in `copy_lanes`, and the router says it where it starts.
