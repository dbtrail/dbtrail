### Changed

- PostgreSQL port, through a forwarding account: a name a statement defines itself (`SUM(x) AS units`, a column of a `WITH` or a subquery, read as `s.units`) is judged by what it stands for when the port decides whether the copy may answer. A count, a sum, an average, a rank, arithmetic, a `CASE` that answers only numbers, or a number column renamed is not text, so comparing or sorting it no longer sends the statement to the source; a qualified name (`d.d_next_o_id`) is judged by its column, not by its table's name; and `r.rnk` in `WHERE` is a column of `r`, not an alias of the select list. Nine of the fourteen reports of a TPC-C analytics workload now stay on the copy (three before). The same reading now sends to the source a statement that sorts by a text column of a `WITH` through its table's name (`ORDER BY s.label`), which the copy used to answer in byte order.
