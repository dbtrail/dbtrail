- **SQL on the copy of a PostgreSQL source reads `time`, `timetz` and `interval` as their types** (#2787). The three were the text PostgreSQL wrote, so they sorted as text and could not be compared with a time or an interval, or added to a date. The views now read `time` and `timetz` as times of day and `interval` as an interval that keeps its months, days and time apart, as PostgreSQL does, mixed signs included. A `time` of `24:00:00`, which PostgreSQL holds and the copy would show as midnight, fails the statement that reads the column; a `timetz` of `24:00:00` reads as it is. Arrays of the three stay text. See [how each type reads](postgres.md#how-each-type-reads-in-sql-on-the-copy).
