- **Docs: what fresh mode gives and costs on the PostgreSQL port, as measured** (part of #2756). `docs/postgres.md` now says, from a measurement on a PostgreSQL 16 source with tables of 2 and 10 million rows: an answer in fresh mode is about 1.2 seconds behind the source and never more than 3.2; a statement costs 2 to 3 times what it costs in fast mode, and the table's size counts for more than the mode; a statement that reads one row by its key reads the whole table unless a forwarding user sends it to the source; and switching a server from fresh to fast makes the connections already open read an answer older than their last one, which they are told once by a NOTICE.
