- **Engine Routing no longer answers a date stepped past the year 9999 or before the year 1** (#2800). `DATE_ADD(dt, INTERVAL 1 DAY)`, `ADDDATE`, `dt + INTERVAL 1 DAY` and `dt - INTERVAL 1 DAY` went to the copy as written, and where the result left the years 1 to 9999 the copy computed a year of five digits or the year 0 where MySQL answers `NULL`: a client saw a value MySQL never sends (some drivers failed with `bad value for field`), and a `WHERE` or a `COUNT` over such a step kept rows MySQL leaves out. The routing port now sends each step inside a function of the copy's session that checks the range, as `DATE_SUB` does since #2799, so such a statement is answered by MySQL; inside the range the copy's answer is unchanged, to the last second of 9999. `ADDDATE` with an `INTERVAL` is now the copy's too. A step the port cannot send that way with certainty (the `INTERVAL` first, a negative amount without quotes, a decimal amount, a placeholder, arithmetic right before the operand or after the unit) stays on MySQL. `NOW()` and the other clocks stepped by less than a thousand years are sent as before. The port without routing is unchanged. See [date functions under routing](time-travel-sql.md).
