- **The PostgreSQL port's join check no longer sends a quick report to the source on a small overestimate** (#3287). A join is now flagged only when its estimate passes both 100 times its larger input and 3 billion rows. Under a write load, CH-benCHmark Q3 was sometimes estimated at 1 billion rows and answered by the source in about 60 s; it answers on the copy in about 6 s.
