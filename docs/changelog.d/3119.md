- **The PostgreSQL router starts when its status address is taken** (#3119). `bintrail-console pg-router` exited with "the PostgreSQL router cannot serve its status on ..." when it could not listen on `--status-addr` (`BINTRAIL_PG_ROUTER_STATUS_ADDR`; 127.0.0.1:9092 in the packaged unit, :9092 in the compose file), so anything else holding that address kept the PostgreSQL port closed while the service restarted over and over. It now prints a `WARNING` at start-up, writes it to its log, and serves the port without `/status` and `/metrics`, as the MySQL router does with its numbers.
