- **The PostgreSQL port keeps one of the copy's workers for short statements** (#PR). With reports holding every worker, a short statement for the copy waited behind them: a sum of one warehouse, a second alone, took 13 to 31 seconds under 30 connections. A statement whose plan on the source reads one table and joins nothing now takes any free worker, and one is always left for such statements; the others, and every statement with no plan, run at most `--workers` minus 1 at once. "One table" is counted on the safe side: a subquery, a `WITH`, a partitioned table or a self-join is not short. With `--workers 1` nothing changes. `/status` says how it stands in `copy_lanes`, and the router says it where it starts.
