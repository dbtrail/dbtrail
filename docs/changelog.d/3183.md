- **Fresh mode no longer answers from the snapshot alone, with no reason, when a table's tail is published at the wrong instant** (#3183, closes #3088). A statement reads the tails, then what was published, then each table's state; an update that published between two of those reads made them disagree, and the statement answered from the snapshot without waiting, without routing to the source and without a warning, only its lag showing the answer was old. It now looks again when an update published during its reads. MySQL and PostgreSQL sources alike.
