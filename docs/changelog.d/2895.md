- **`dbtrail port` knows the PostgreSQL port** (#2895, part of #2773). With PostgreSQL servers, `port on` and `port status` print the `psql` line for the PostgreSQL port (5433, the server's name as the database) in place of the mysql line and Engine Routing. `port routing on`, `port mode` and `port max-behind` say why they do not apply there (the copy answers alone) and change nothing. With MySQL or MariaDB servers the commands print what they did.
