### Added

- PostgreSQL port, through a forwarding account: `extract(epoch from x)` of a timestamp and `to_timestamp(n)` of a whole number of seconds are answered on the copy as PostgreSQL answers them (the epoch as a `numeric` with six decimals, from the copy's whole microseconds), so time buckets written `to_timestamp(floor(extract(epoch from x) / 600) * 600)` stay on the copy. An epoch of a date or an interval, and `to_timestamp` of a fraction of a second, are refused and go to the source. Thirteen of the fourteen reports of a TPC-C analytics workload now stay on the copy; the fourteenth sorts text, which goes to the source unless the database orders text by bytes.
