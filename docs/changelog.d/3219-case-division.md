- **PostgreSQL port: a share of a total, `sum(CASE WHEN ... THEN amount ELSE 0 END) / sum(amount)`, is answered by the copy** (#3219). A division with a `CASE` on a side went to the source, because the port could not tell from the statement whether the `CASE` was a `numeric`. It now reads the `CASE` by its values, as PostgreSQL types it: the values after `THEN` and `ELSE`, not its conditions, with a `NULL` among them taking the others' type. CH-benCHmark's Q14 (`100.00 * sum(CASE WHEN i_data LIKE 'PR%' THEN ol_amount ELSE 0 END) / (1 + sum(ol_amount))`) is now the copy's, and so is the division of Q8; Q8 itself still goes to the source for its `char(n)` columns. The quotient is the copy's as for any division since #3238: PostgreSQL's number rounded to 16 decimals. Still sent to the source: a `CASE` of whole numbers over a whole number (`sum(CASE WHEN c THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) / count(*)`, which PostgreSQL divides as whole numbers), a `CASE` with a float, a `bigint` summed, a `numeric` without precision, or only `NULL` among its values. A divisor that is zero in some row is PostgreSQL's error, answered by the source.
